# Meeting prep (`meeting-prep`)

Prepares you for one meeting on your calendar. Ask "prep me for my 10:30" or "what do I need for the
call with Meridian", and Claude tells you who is in the room, what the meeting is about, what was
agreed last time and is still open, what is in flight with these people, and the two or three things
worth deciding or asking.

## What it needs

Connect these under **Customize > Connectors**, with your own accounts:

- **Google Calendar**, to find the meeting.
- **Gmail** and **Google Drive**, for recent threads, shared documents and meeting notes saved there.
- Optional: **Granola** or **Zoom** for notes from earlier meetings, and **Slack**.

Any of these can be missing. Claude says which ones it couldn't reach, so you know what the
preparation is missing.

## What it does with your data

It only reads. It doesn't send, draft, create or change anything. It reads the accounts you
connected, so it prepares you from your own mail and calendar, not anyone else's.
