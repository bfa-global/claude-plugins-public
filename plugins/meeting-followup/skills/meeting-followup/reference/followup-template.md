# Meeting follow-up template

Two parts. **Part one always. Part two only when the user asked for something to go out.**

"Write it up" is a request for part one. "Draft the email", "send the recap", or naming a
recipient is a request for both. When part two is not asked for, end after the read with the
offer, and that is a complete answer rather than a truncated one.

The worked example is fictional (Meridian Foundation as funder, Baraka as delivery partner).

---

## Part one: the read

### [Meeting title], [day and date]

[Attendees, from the calendar event.]

**Agreed** ([n])

[Every commitment with an owner and a date. The user's own items first.]

*Worked example:*

> - **You**: send Daniel the June variance note, by Wednesday 2 September.
> - **You**: confirm whether BFA can host the September session, no date agreed.
> - **Grace**: circulate the venue contract, by Friday 4 September.
> - **Daniel**: take the renewal term back to his board, by their meeting on 9 September.

**Open** ([n])

[Raised and not answered, decisions deferred, anything left hanging. These are what the
follow-up chases.]

*Worked example:*

> - Whether the renewal is one year or three. Raised twice, not decided.
> - Who covers facilitator costs if the session moves to Nairobi. Nobody picked it up.

**Not carried forward**

[One line naming what was discussed and produced nothing, so the user can see it was read.]

*Worked example:*

> Also discussed: the 2027 reporting calendar, and Grace's update on the Kisumu cohort.
> Neither produced an action.

**Already done**

[Anything agreed in the room that the mail shows has since happened. Drop this section when
there is nothing.]

*Worked example:*

> The results table Daniel asked for was already sent on 12 August, so it is not in the draft.

---

## Part two: the draft

**Only when asked for.** Addressed to the attendees, in the user's register. Short. No
preamble. Nothing is sent.

*Worked example:*

> **Subject:** Meridian check-in, what we agreed
>
> Hi Daniel, Grace,
>
> Quick recap of this afternoon so we all have the same list.
>
> On me: the June variance note to Daniel by Wednesday. I will also confirm whether we can
> host the September session, though we did not put a date on that one, so tell me if you
> need it sooner.
>
> Grace, you are circulating the venue contract by Friday. Daniel, you are taking the
> renewal term to your board on the 9th.
>
> Two things we did not close. Whether the renewal is one year or three, which came up twice
> and we left open. And who covers facilitator costs if the session moves to Nairobi.
>
> Happy to pick both up on a call before the 9th if that helps.

---

**Coverage.** [Meeting], notes from [Granola or Zoom], [N] mail threads checked.

When part two was written: nothing has been sent. Want me to adjust the tone, add the next
meeting date, or put this in Gmail as a draft for you to send?

When it was not: want me to draft the email or a Slack post for this?

---

## Notes on tone

The recap goes to people who were in the room, so accuracy outranks polish and an invented
action is caught immediately. Lead with what the user owes. Use the names people used, not
email addresses. Record decisions as decided, because a recap that reports discussion without
the decision is how a decision gets relitigated a fortnight later. Never put a name against
something that person did not accept. No em dashes, no en dashes.
